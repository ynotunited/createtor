# Createtor — Brand Identity

**Tagline:** *create by creators*
**Positioning:** The platform where creators build, publish, and get paid — built by people who understand creators.

---

## 1. Brand Core

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Essence** | Creators, equipped. |
| **Mission** | Give creators the tools to build, publish, and monetize their work without friction. |
| **Vision** | Become the default home for independent creators across Africa and beyond. |
| **Promise** | Your work, your audience, your terms. |

### Brand Personality

- **Bold** — confident, not loud. Makes a statement without shouting.
- **Warm** — human, approachable, rooted in the maker's workshop.
- **Crafted** — precise and considered. Quality is visible in details.
- **Accessible** — built for everyone, not just the technically elite.

### We Are / We Are Not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Direct and clear | Corporate or stiff |
| Encouraging of creators | Hype-driven or cheesy |
| Practical about tools and money | Vague about pricing or features |

### Voice Guidelines

- Speak to creators as peers, not students.
- Short sentences. Active verbs. Specific nouns.
- Celebrate the work, not the platform.

**Good:** *"Publish today. Get paid this week."*
**Avoid:** *"Leverage our revolutionary ecosystem to unlock your creative potential!"*

## 2. Logo

### Primary Logo
- Oxblood **C mark** + **Createtor** wordmark (Oxblood `#710000`)
- Tagline **create by creators** in Ochre `#c36f09`, letter-spaced

### Logo Variants

| Variant | Use case |
|---|---|
| Primary (full colour) | Default. Light backgrounds. |
| Reversed (gold/white) | Dark backgrounds, Oxblood or Ink surfaces |
| Monochrome (Ink or White) | Single-colour print, embossing, fax-style applications |
| Icon only (C mark) | Favicon, app icon, social avatars, loaders |
| Wordmark only | Tight horizontal spaces where the tagline won't fit |

### Clear Space
Keep a minimum clear space equal to the **height of the lowercase "e"** in *Createtor* on all sides.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: **120 px** wide (full lockup) / **24 px** (icon only)
- Print: **30 mm** wide (full lockup) / **8 mm** (icon only)

### Logo Don'ts
- Don't recolour the logo outside the approved palette.
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate, or add effects (shadows, outlines, glows).
- Don't place it on busy photography without a solid backing panel.
- Don't rearrange the tagline or change its letter-spacing.
- Don't use the C mark alone as a substitute for the full logo in marketing headers.

## 3. Colour Palette

Five brand colours, named by character:

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oxblood** | `#710000` | 113, 0, 0 | Primary. Logo, headings, primary surfaces. |
| **Forge** | `#a63c06` | 166, 60, 6 | Secondary. Hover states, dividers, depth. |
| **Ochre** | `#c36f09` | 195, 111, 9 | Accent text (large), tagline, icons, highlights. |
| **Gold** | `#eeba0b` | 238, 186, 11 | Primary CTA fills, badges, success highlights. |
| **Spark** | `#f4e409` | 244, 228, 9 | Energy accent. Use sparingly: illustrations, callouts, on dark. |

### Neutrals

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper** | `#FCF8F3` | Default page background (warm off-white) |
| **Sand** | `#F3E9DF` | Cards, section backgrounds |
| **Line** | `#E9DED5` | Borders, dividers |
| **Stone** | `#6E5D57` | Secondary text, captions |
| **Ink** | `#1F1512` | Body text, dark mode background |

### Colour Proportion (60-30-10)
- **60%** Paper / Sand / Ink (neutrals)
- **30%** Oxblood + Forge (brand weight)
- **10%** Gold, Ochre, Spark (energy and action)

### Accessibility — Approved Pairings

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.1 calculated values.

| Foreground | Background | Ratio | Status | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oxblood `#710000` | Paper `#FCF8F3` | ~12:1 | AAA | All body and headings |
| Forge `#a63c06` | Paper | ~6.4:1 | AA | Links, secondary text |
| Ochre `#c36f09` | Paper | ~3.7:1 | Large text only | Headings ≥24px, tagline, icons |
| White `#FFFFFF` | Oxblood | ~12.4:1 | AAA | Text on primary buttons |
| Gold `#eeba0b` | Oxblood | ~6.9:1 | AA | Text/icons on dark brand surfaces |
| Spark `#f4e409` | Ink `#1F1512` | ~16:1 | AAA | Highlights on dark mode |
| Ink `#1F1512` | Gold `#eeba0b` | ~11:1 | AAA | Text on CTA buttons |

### ⚠️ Colour Warnings (Do Not Ignore)
- **Gold and Spark fail contrast on light backgrounds** (~1.8:1 and ~1.4:1 on Paper). Never use them for text or thin lines on white.
- **Ochre fails for body text** (~3.7:1 on Paper). Limit it to large headings, icons, and the tagline.
- **Pair Gold CTAs with Ink text, not white.** White on Gold fails.

## 4. Gradients

- **Ember** (hero and banners): `linear-gradient(135deg, #710000 0%, #a63c06 55%, #c36f09 100%)`
- **Spark** (highlights, progress, badges): `linear-gradient(90deg, #eeba0b 0%, #f4e409 100%)`

Rules: gradients are for backgrounds and graphics only, never the logo. Keep text on gradients at white or Gold with enough contrast.

## 5. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headings** | **Outfit** | 500, 600, 700 | Geometric, matches the wordmark's character |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter** | 400, 500, 600 | Highly legible at small sizes |
| **Tagline / Labels** | **Outfit** | 300 | Letter-spacing 0.2–0.3em, lowercase or uppercase |
| **Code / Mono** | **JetBrains Mono** | 400, 500 | Developer docs and technical content |

### Type Scale (web)

| Token | Size / Line height | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| `display` | 56 / 60 px | Hero headlines |
| `h1` | 40 / 44 px | Page titles |
| `h2` | 30 / 36 px | Section titles |
| `h3` | 22 / 28 px | Card titles |
| `body` | 16 / 26 px | Default text |
| `small` | 14 / 20 px | Captions, metadata |
| `label` | 12 / 16 px, uppercase, +0.12em | Eyebrows, badges |

## 6. Iconography & Imagery

- **Icons:** 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins. Use Ochre or Oxblood. Lucide or Phosphor style is the reference.
- **Shapes:** Use the C mark's geometry (rounded chevron, soft angles) as the basis for decorative shapes and corner accents.
- **Photography:** Real creators at work. Warm natural light, African subjects in authentic settings, no stock-clichés. Apply a subtle warm tint (+5% red, −5% blue) for consistency.
- **Illustration:** Flat shapes, two to three palette colours per piece, no outlines.

## 8. Applications

- **Website / Web app:** Paper background, Oxblood headings, Gold primary CTAs.
- **Dashboard:** Sand surfaces, Oxblood for active nav, Spark for positive metrics.
- **Social media:** Oxblood or Ember backgrounds with Gold headlines. Alternate with Paper posts.
- **Email:** Single-column, Oxblood header with reversed logo, Gold CTA.
- **Print:** Monochrome logo on uncoated stock. Oxblood as the default ink.
- **Merch:** Reversed logo on Oxblood or Ink. Limit to two colours per item.

## 10. Strategic Notes & Risks

- **Name check:** "Createtor" is close to "Creator" and "Creatör". Run a trademark search in Nigeria (CAC/NIPO) and globally (USPTO / WIPO) before launch.
- **Contrast risk:** The bright palette (Gold, Spark) is attractive but fails on light backgrounds. Enforce the pairing rules in code review, not just in the guide.
- **Ochre tagline:** Fine at tagline size, but if the logo is shown at small sizes on white, the tagline may disappear. Provide a wordmark-only version for those cases.
- **Dark mode:** Gold becomes the primary action colour in dark mode. Test that CTAs still stand out against Ink.
- **Gradient overuse:** Keep the Ember gradient to hero and feature moments. If it appears everywhere, the brand loses its punch.
